# Ground Up Design Brief

## 1. Starting point
- Starting point(s): My own design (a Figma mockup of the home page, desktop and mobile)
- Mood in three words: earthy, fun, warm
- Notes: No health elitism. A visitor should feel welcome in a few seconds.

## 2. Colors
- Brand: forest green #2E6F40 (all body text and headings)
- Accent: blue #40a2ce (the keyboard focus ring)
- Text: brown #6b350e on white
- Background: white #ffffff cards on a teal #35667c page; dark brown #4a2409 as the shadow behind white headlines and the fallback color behind page photos

## 3. Type
- Headings: Nunito, weight 900 (Google Fonts)
- Body: Nunito, regular and semi-bold weights (Google Fonts)
- License: Google Fonts, free for web use

## 5. Spacing and components
- Spacing: sections 60 to 80px apart (grows with screen width); content no wider than 1052px
- Corners: 20px on cards and photos, 11px on the hero photo
- Primary action: the two menu cards (a photo with a white label across the bottom)
- Navigation: white text links (Drinks, Food, About Us) in a strip over the hero photo

## 6. Layout: Home page, top to bottom
1. Navigation: logo and three links over the hero photo
2. Hero: a photo with rounded corners and the headline "No highfalutin coffee snobbery, just good coffee"
3. Intro: "Your friendly neighborhood", "Coffee Shop in Williamsburg, Brooklyn", "Open Daily 7am to 8pm"
4. Three feature rows: a photo beside a short paragraph, alternating sides
5. Two menu cards: Drink Menu, Food Menu
6. Footer: phone, address, email, social icons

Rhythm: the same gap between every section; the photo side alternates to keep it lively
Grid break: none, on purpose; the alternating photo side provides the variety

## 7. Responsive rules
Phone: one column. Navigation stacks (logo above the links); the hero headline sits over the photo; the three feature rows stack with the photo above its text; the two menu cards stack; footer details stack with the address first.

From 760px: navigation in one row over the photo; hero photo inset with rounded corners; the two menu cards side by side; on the About page the three info cards (Hours, Find Us, Say Hello) go three across.

From 900px: the three feature rows put the photo beside the text, alternating sides; the footer shows phone, address and email in one row.

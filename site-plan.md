# Pregustator Site Plan

## 1. Goal

- Kind of site: Informational  (ingredient comparison)
- Positioning: A company (app)
- Audience: Health conscious people as well as those that don't trust FDA standards
- The main thing I want a visitor to do is: determine the differences and keep track of substandard food products.

## 2. References

### Pregustator (insert-actual-site-name.vercel.app)
- Patterns: top nav (How It Works, Track the Poison, Your Bans, Top Toxins, About Us), hero, repeating text sections, footer with phone, address, email
- Structure: hero (headline) > three short sections > Test for Poison card > footer
- Content: says what it is, what it does, and where the information comes from; no jargon
- Primary action: the Test card
- Why it works: a visitor knows what this place is within a few seconds

## 3. Sitemap

- Home: what we are, three reasons to use, links to the ingredient checker
- How it Works: breakdown of the methodology used (sources, differences between regulations, what the site does and doesn't do)
- Track the Poison: the ingredient checker(user lookup of products/ingredients in the US that in the EU are banned/have warnings/have health risks, Gus the Tester gives breakdown)
- About Us: who we are, our goals
- No Contact page: phone, address and email live in the footer of every page

## 4. Primary actions and main flow

| Page | Primary action |
| --- | --- |
| Home | See the checker (Track the Poison card) |
| How It Works | Today: none in the main content. Suggest: Track the Poison (button link to Track the Poison page) |
| Track the Poison | Products/ingredient lookup. |
| Top Warnings| Today: none in the main content. Suggest: Track the Poison (button link to Track the Poison page) |
| About Us | Call us (the phone link) |

Main flow: Home > Track the Poison > footer details > find an alternative product
